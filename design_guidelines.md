# Scent Atelier - Design Guidelines

## Design Approach
**Luxury Perfume Brand Experience** - An elegant, image-driven website that feels like walking through an art gallery of scents. The design emphasizes sophistication, visual storytelling, and emotional connection through refined aesthetics.

## Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- Cream White: `#FFF8F2` (main background)
- Gold: `#CDAA7D` (accents, borders, CTAs)
- Charcoal Gray: `#333333` (primary text)

**Accent Colors:**
- Soft Pink: `#EACBC3` (subtle highlights)
- Coffee Brown: `#5A3E36` (secondary elements)

## Typography

**Headings:** Playfair Display (elegant serif) - for all titles, product names, page headers
**Body Text:** Poppins or Open Sans (clean sans-serif) - for descriptions, paragraphs, form labels
**Sizes:** Use hierarchical scaling - large display text for hero sections, medium for subheadings, comfortable reading size for body

## Layout System

**Spacing:** Use generous padding and margins throughout to create breathing room
**Grid:** Product layouts use elegant grid/table structures with consistent spacing
**Containers:** Content centered with max-width constraints for readability
**Navigation:** Fixed or sticky navigation bar with links to all pages (Home | Products | About | Adverts | Shop | Contact)

## Component Library

### Navigation
- Elegant horizontal navigation bar with brand logo centered at top
- Clean text links with hover states using gold accent
- Consistent across all pages

### Product Display Tables
```
Structured table layout with columns:
- Product image (thumbnail)
- Product Name (linked with anchors)
- Type (category)
- Price (Ksh format)
- Description (brief, evocative)
```

### Forms (Shop/Contact Page)
- Order form with fields: Name, Email, Phone, Product Selection (dropdown), Quantity, Delivery Address
- Shipping fees table below form
- Submit button in gold accent color
- Success message after submission

### Marquee Elements
- Home page: Welcome message with brand tagline
- Adverts page: Limited edition announcements
- Smooth scrolling animation

### Frames
- Side promotional frames showing special offers ("Buy 2 Get 1 Free")
- Embedded within product or advertisement pages

### Cards/Sections
- Clean section divisions with background images
- Each page has distinct background (perfume bottles, flowers, workshop scenes, elegant textures)
- Overlay text with sufficient contrast for readability

## Images

**Required Image Types:**
1. **Logo:** "Scent Atelier" wordmark - elegant, minimal
2. **Home Background:** Blurred perfume bottle with soft, dreamy lighting
3. **Product Images:** Individual perfume bottles (L'Amour, Noir Essence, Soleil) - clean product photography
4. **About Background:** Perfume workshop/atelier scene - artisanal, craftsmanship feel
5. **About Supporting:** Designer mixing scents - storytelling image
6. **Advertisement:** Campaign poster/mockup - dark elegant gradient background
7. **Shop Background:** Minimal perfume shelf or elegant counter setting
8. **Decorative:** Flowers, essential oils, elegant textures for various sections

**Image Treatment:**
- Soft overlays to ensure text readability
- Consistent lighting and mood across all images
- High-quality, professional product photography aesthetic

**Hero Sections:** Each page uses a large hero or background image establishing the mood

## Page-Specific Guidelines

### Home Page
- Centered logo and tagline
- Marquee announcement bar
- Short brand introduction paragraph with cream background overlay
- "Explore Collection" CTA button in gold
- Background: Soft-focus perfume imagery

### Products Page
- Elegant table layout with alternating subtle row backgrounds
- Product images left-aligned within table cells
- Internal anchor links on product names
- Light gradient or floral pattern background
- Clear pricing in consistent format

### About Page
- Storytelling layout with background image
- Brand story paragraph with elegant typography
- Workshop image showcasing craftsmanship
- Links back to Home and Products

### Advertisement Page
- Dark elegant gradient background
- Campaign title in large Playfair Display
- Embedded video or poster mockup (centered, prominent)
- Marquee for latest offers
- Descriptive campaign paragraph

### Shop/Contact Page
- Clean form layout with generous spacing
- Dropdown product selector matching product page offerings
- Shipping table below form
- Background: Minimal, sophisticated setting
- Clear submission feedback

## Animation & Interactions

- Smooth page transitions
- Subtle hover effects on navigation and product links (gold underline or color shift)
- Marquee smooth scrolling
- Form validation feedback
- No excessive or distracting animations - maintain elegance

## Footer

All pages include:
- Student name and registration number
- Copyright notice: "© 2025 Scent Atelier. Crafted by [Your Name]."
- Minimal, unobtrusive design in charcoal or coffee brown text

## Overall Aesthetic

**Mood:** Luxurious, artistic, sophisticated, intimate
**Feel:** Like browsing a high-end perfume boutique or art gallery
**Tone:** Elegant, refined, emotive, premium quality
**Balance:** Image-rich storytelling balanced with clean, readable content presentation
