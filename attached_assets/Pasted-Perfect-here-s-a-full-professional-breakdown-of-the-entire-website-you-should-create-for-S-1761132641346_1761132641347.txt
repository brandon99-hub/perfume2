## 🌸 PROJECT TITLE: SCENT ATELIER – The Art of Fragrance

---

### 🧭 **1. Website Overview**

**Scent Atelier** is a luxurious, modern perfume brand website designed to showcase handcrafted fragrances, recent product campaigns, and provide an online platform for customers to explore and make inquiries or purchases.

The website is elegant, image-driven, and uses visual storytelling to convey sophistication — as if the visitor is walking through an art gallery of scents.

---

### 📑 **2. Pages to Include**

#### 🏠 **a) Home Page**

**Purpose:** To welcome visitors and introduce the brand identity.
**Content & Features:**

* Brand logo “Scent Atelier” at the top center.
* Navigation bar with links: *Home | Products | About | Adverts | Shop | Contact*
* Marquee text scrolling across:
  *“Welcome to Scent Atelier — Experience the Art of Fragrance | Discover Your Signature Scent Today!”*
* Background image: a blurred perfume bottle with soft lighting.
* Short intro paragraph:

  > “Scent Atelier is a fragrance studio dedicated to crafting exquisite, emotion-driven perfumes. Each scent is a masterpiece — designed to evoke memories and moments.”
* A button linking to the products page (*“Explore Collection”*).

---

#### 🧴 **b) Products Page**

**Purpose:** To display different perfumes offered.
**Content & Features:**

* Elegant product grid or table layout with images, names, and short descriptions.
* Example table:

| Product Name | Type   | Price     | Description                            |
| ------------ | ------ | --------- | -------------------------------------- |
| L’Amour      | Floral | Ksh 5,500 | A blend of rose, jasmine, and vanilla. |
| Noir Essence | Woody  | Ksh 6,800 | Deep, musky, and mysterious.           |
| Soleil       | Citrus | Ksh 4,900 | Bright, fresh, and full of sunshine.   |

* Background: light gradient or subtle floral pattern.
* Internal links on product names leading to detailed sections using **anchors**.
* Optional: use frames to show side promotions (like “Buy 2 Get 1 Free”).

---

#### 🧠 **c) About Page**

**Purpose:** To tell the brand story.
**Content:**

* Background: elegant image of a perfume workshop (atelier).
* Text:

  > “Founded in 2025, Scent Atelier emerged from a passion for olfactory art. Each bottle is handcrafted with precision, blending tradition and innovation to create timeless fragrances.”
* Include an image of a perfume designer mixing scents.
* Link back to Home and Products.

---

#### 📰 **d) Advertisement Page**

**Purpose:** To showcase the company’s latest promotional campaign.
**Content & Features:**

* Title: *“The Essence of Confidence – New 2025 Campaign.”*
* Embedded video or poster (Photoshop-designed ad mockup).
* Background: dark elegant gradient.
* A short paragraph describing the ad:

  > “Our new ad celebrates individuality and expression. Scent Atelier – for those who wear their story.”
* Include a marquee announcing the latest offers:
  *“Limited Edition: Noir Essence now available in exclusive 50ml bottles!”*

---

#### 💳 **e) Online Transactions / Contact Page**

**Purpose:** To simulate an online purchase or contact form.
**Content & Features:**

* Title: *“Shop or Get in Touch.”*

* Background image of a shopping counter or minimal perfume shelf.

* A form for customers to place an order:

  **Order Form:**

  * Name
  * Email
  * Phone Number
  * Select Product (dropdown)
  * Quantity
  * Delivery Address
  * Submit button

* Use a **table** below to show estimated shipping fees.

* After form submission, show a message (using simple JavaScript alert or link):
  *“Thank you for your interest! Our team will reach out to confirm your order.”*
